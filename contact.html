FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for one of your programmes?

Visit our Get Involved page and fill in the programme application form. Our team will contact you within 3 working days.

Are your programmes free to join?

Most of our programmes are fully funded or subsidised for eligible participants. Scholarships and grants are available — speak to our team for details.

How can my company become a partner?

Send us a message using the Partnership tab on the form above or email us directly. We work with corporate partners, government bodies, and international NGOs.

Can I volunteer even if I live outside Nigeria?

Absolutely. We welcome volunteers from the Nigerian diaspora and international professionals. Many mentoring and training roles can be done remotely.

How do I make a donation?

Visit our Donate page to give online via bank transfer, card, or mobile money. All donations are acknowledged with a receipt.

Is the Samson Soyebi Foundation a registered NGO?

Yes. The Samson Soyebi Foundation is a registered non-governmental organisation operating in Nigeria and the United Kingdom, fully compliant with applicable laws.
