Our Story

Rooted in Purpose,
Driven by Impact

Founded by Builder Samson Soyebi entrepreneur, economist, and passionate advocate for Nigerian youth, the Samson Soyebi Foundation was born from a deeply personal belief: that every young person in Africa deserves a fair shot at success.

From vocational training in Lagos to international education pathways, we walk alongside young Nigerians at every stage. equipping them with skills, networks, and the confidence to thrive.
